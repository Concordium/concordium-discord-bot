# Privacy Policy for Concordium Discord Bot

Last updated: June 2026

## Overview

The Concordium Discord Bot is a community verification bot used within Discord servers related to the Concordium ecosystem.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the bot collects, how it is used, and how it is stored.

## Information We Collect

The bot may collect and store the following information when users voluntarily participate in verification processes:

### Discord Data

* Discord User ID
* Discord Server ID
* Discord Role assignments related to verification

### Verification Data

Depending on the verification method used, the bot may collect:

* Concordium account addresses
* Validator identifiers
* Delegation information
* GitHub profile information submitted for developer verification
* Verification transaction hashes
* Verification timestamps

### Preferences

* Notification preferences chosen by the user

## How We Use This Information

The collected information is used solely for:

* Verifying a user's relationship to the Concordium ecosystem
* Assigning and maintaining Discord roles
* Managing verification status
* Supporting community features related to verified members
* Maintaining the integrity and security of the verification system

The bot does not use collected information for advertising, marketing, profiling, or analytics purposes.

## Message Content

The bot may temporarily process message content when users participate in verification workflows.

Message content is used only to extract the information required for verification, such as wallet addresses, validator identifiers, or other verification-related data.

The bot does not store general Discord conversations or messages beyond what is necessary to maintain verification records.

## Data Storage

Verification-related information is stored in a database operated by the Concordium administrators.

Data is retained only for as long as necessary to provide verification functionality and maintain role assignments.

## Data Sharing

The bot does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

Information may only be accessed by authorized administrators responsible for operating and maintaining the verification system.

## User Choices

Participation in the verification process is entirely voluntary.

Users who do not wish to provide verification information may choose not to participate in verification workflows.

Users may request removal of their verification records by contacting the server administrators.

## Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect stored verification information from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure.

However, no system can guarantee absolute security.
